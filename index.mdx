Wirebox equips autonomous AI agents with real-world identities, dedicated email inboxes, and real-time webhook delivery.

Agents are no longer isolated LLM loops. With Wirebox, your agents can sign up for services, receive verification codes, reply to human customers, and exchange emails with the outside world.

---

## Key Capabilities

- [**Agent Identities**](/capabilities/identities) — Claim unique handles (`@eva`) and attach email, phone, and iMessage channels under distinct identities.
- [**Email**](/capabilities/email) — Provisioned inboxes with drafts, search, forwarding, and allow/block guardrails — sending from `wireboxmail.com` or your own domain.
- [**Phone**](/capabilities/phone) — Dedicated phone numbers for inbound and outbound SMS and MMS.
- [**iMessage**](/capabilities/imessage) — Reach people in the native Messages app through the Wirebox router line.
- [**Tunnel**](/capabilities/tunnels) — Give any local agent process a public HTTPS and WebSocket address with zero port forwarding.
- [**Webhooks**](/capabilities/webhooks) — Signed, real-time deliveries for email, SMS, and iMessage events, with HMAC verification and secret rotation.
